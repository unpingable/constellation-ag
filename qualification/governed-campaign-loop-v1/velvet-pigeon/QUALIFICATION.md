## Frozen law and schemas

`ag.external-evidence-reservation/v1` binds only campaign, stage/ordinal, logical attempt, exact initial or prior-reservation predecessor, result constraints, exact successor or terminal human stop, worker-session manifest, executor/NQ templates, instruction/mutation/resource profiles, and eight explicit nonclaims. Its ID is SHA-256 over a closed canonical preimage with the identity field empty.

`ag.governed-campaign.packet/v1` contains exactly three ordered stages and three distinct reservations. Stage 2/3 predecessors cite the exact prior stage and R instead of predicting future Git objects. Stage 3 is `human_required`; a Stage 4 is not representable.

Executor templates freeze the runtime schema, tagged predecessor coordinate, session/profile/artifact identities, mutation and resource bounds, and one empty construction coordinate, `evidence_reservation`. They contain no future Porter, candidate, result, or settlement identity. Stage 1 materializes its exact literal Git predecessor. Stages 2/3 materialize the exact Git predecessor from the unique realized result named by the already-frozen prior reservation; the tagged prior-R coordinate is validated before materialization and is not a profile wildcard. Reservation-aware W5 plan v2 no longer contains `expected_result_head` or `expected_result_tree`; those remain future factual candidate identities and are validated by host custody. Frozen W5 v1 remains readable with its original fields.

NQ templates freeze the same tagged predecessor coordinate and have two empty construction-time coordinates: the already-predeclared R and the packet's own identity. Those two values are filled after packet sealing; the exact runtime predecessor Git objects are materialized from the frozen predecessor coordinate at each stage before that stage's Docket admission. This resolves content-addressed self-reference and unknowable prior-stage result bytes without retroactive packet/catalog mutation, wildcard matching, or controller-authored qualification.

Frozen V0 packet/schema readers and AG V1 catalogs are unchanged. Docket wire, custody, settlement, authority, and reconciliation law are unchanged.

## Porter, executor, NQ, Nightshift, AG chain

Porter `porter.record.v1` adds one scalar `evidence_reservation`; v0 is unchanged. Fresh random P generation is unchanged. The record hash binds exact P and R.

W5 plan/request/outcome v2 carry R. The SQLite store has unique non-null R and P constraints, claims R before bundle/Porter activity, searches exact R during reconcile, and refuses re-invocation after exact run evidence. The Docket attempt binds the exact plan identity transitively through existing work identity.

NQ `campaign-stage-realization` V2 profile/evidence/receipt binds R, attempt, plan template and realized plan, settlement, P and Porter record hash, executor receipt, predecessor, actual result HEAD/tree, and exact gate/profile evidence. For a prior-reservation predecessor it additionally requires the complete prior NQ profile/evidence/receipt, exact replay, `QUALIFIED`, exactly one prior realization, matching prior R/stage, and exact equality between that result HEAD/tree and the new predecessor. A wrong or forged predecessor is `INDETERMINATE`. Zero or multiple current chains are also `INDETERMINATE`; NQ never selects a favorable chain. Status vocabulary remains exactly `QUALIFIED`, `FAILED`, `INDETERMINATE`.

Nightshift's reservation-applicability v1 replays the exact NQ receipt with the exact evaluator binary. First exact realization binds R; exact replay is idempotent; a different receipt/run is durably retained as conflict and can never be Current. FAILED/INDETERMINATE are retained-only. Stale preserves history.

AG catalog requirements are predeclared as:

`{basis_type: nightshift.repository-qualification-reservation-applicability/v1, basis_identity: R}`

AG remains ignorant of P, gates, settlements, NQ status internals, and realization storage. Existing exact type/identity/occurrence/subject/work/resolver/currentness checks are unchanged.
